Joining

An invitation is required. Open your invitation, or choose Paste invitation in the app. If it has expired, ask the account owner for a new one. Do not send invitation links or access credentials in a support message.

Microphone and camera

In Talk in Text, open Settings and choose Phone permissions. Allow microphone access to speak on a call. Camera access is optional for an audio call.

Translation

Translation starts Off for each new call. Turn it on to see translated captions of the other person’s speech on your screen. The person speaking must first agree to Soniox speech sharing. Your control is independent of the other person’s: turning it Off stops your requested captions, not their captions or the call audio.

Translation may stop after its activation limit or recognized-speech inactivity; tap it again when you need it.

Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the error shown. Please do not include private call recordings, conversation text or access credentials.

Account deletion is available in Settings and permanently disables your access. Disconnect this phone only removes phone access. For other privacy or data requests, use the same email address. Read the privacy information for retained records and shared-history deletion.
