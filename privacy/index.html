Privacy

Updated 11 November 2026

Talk in Text is operated by David John-Henry Atkinson. It connects two invited people for voice and video calls. For privacy questions or requests, email d.atkinson6@me.com.

Calls and translation

LiveKit carries call audio and video. Microphone and camera access use your phone’s permissions. You can turn the camera off and continue an audio call.

Translation starts Off for each call. Your Translation control requests captions of the other person’s speech on your screen. Dave receives English captions of Patty’s speech; Patty receives Thai captions of Dave’s speech.

Each person must explicitly agree before their speech can be sent to Soniox. When a listener turns Translation on, a server worker sends the other person’s microphone audio to Soniox for speech recognition and translation. Turning your Translation control Off stops the processing requested for your screen. It does not switch off the other person’s captions or ordinary call audio.

Translation also switches Off after its activation limit or a period without advancing recognized speech. It does not automatically restart. The app does not offer call recordings or a transcript history. Source speech recognition and translated captions are processed transiently.

A small preview of your own camera can show the translated text the other person is reading. This reuses the same translation; it does not start extra speech processing or make a recording.

Information kept to run the service

The service stores participant and phone-session identifiers, protected authentication records, push tokens, invitation state and call/translation usage records. These support secure access, incoming calls, usage limits and the owner’s usage display. They remain associated with the invited participants.

Your phone keeps its access credentials in the protected system keychain. The app reads clipboard text when you choose Paste invitation. It does not upload your address book or ask for location access.

We do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not operate an analytics or advertising script on these pages.

Service providers

  • LiveKit transports media and maintains service connection, quality and usage information. This app does not request media recording.
  • Soniox processes enabled speech in the selected US region. Its real-time API policy describes transient content processing without content retention or model training, and retention of operational metadata such as request identifiers, durations and usage.
  • Apple’s push service delivers incoming-call notifications using a device token and call details, not call audio or captions.
  • Tailscale carries control requests to the hosted service. Network and service providers may retain operational information needed for security and reliability.

Processing may take place outside your country. Traffic uses encrypted transport. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption: the translation worker must access enabled speech.

This website

These privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages information.

Retention and your choices

Device access currently expires after 90 days and invitations after 15 minutes. Expiry does not automatically erase every server record. The usage display keeps up to 50 call summaries; quota reservation details can remain until account deletion.

Disconnect this phone revokes that phone’s active access and removes its saved setup. It does not erase all account or usage records. Account deletion in Settings permanently disables your identity, removes its phone sessions, push tokens and invitations, and removes the pair’s shared detailed call and translation usage history.

Aggregate usage totals remain so deletion cannot reset the service allowance. A minimal revoked-identity record remains to prevent deleted access from being restored with old credentials. A protected deletion-retry receipt expires after 24 hours and is removed by service maintenance, or on the next startup if the host was offline. Provider billing and operational records are subject to those providers’ retention policies.

For access, correction or further deletion questions, email d.atkinson6@me.com. We will explain any records that must be retained before completing the request.

You can change microphone and camera access in your phone’s settings. You can stop translation independently of the call.
